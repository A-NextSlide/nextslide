# Investment Banking Chart Examples - Quick Reference

**For use in DETAILED MODE presentations**

## 🎯 When to Use Detailed Mode

Select **"Detailed Analysis"** mode for:
- Financial analysis and due diligence
- Market research reports  
- Investment memos
- Board presentations
- Strategic planning decks
- Quarterly business reviews

## 📊 Complex Chart Types & Examples

### 1. Waterfall Charts
**Best for:** Revenue bridges, cost breakdowns, value creation

```
Revenue Waterfall 2024: $450M → $520M

Starting Revenue: $450M
+ New Customer Acquisition: +$85M  
+ Expansion Revenue: +$42M
- Customer Churn: -$35M
+ Price Optimization: +$18M
- Operating Costs: -$40M
= Ending Revenue: $520M
```

### 2. Radar/Spider Charts
**Best for:** Competitive analysis, multi-dimensional comparisons

```
Competitive Positioning: 5 Players × 8 Key Metrics

Players: Tesla, GM, Ford, Toyota, VW
Metrics:
- Innovation Score (0-100)
- Market Share (%)
- Customer Satisfaction
- Production Capacity
- R&D Investment
- Brand Value
- Sustainability Rating
- Financial Health
```

### 3. Heatmaps
**Best for:** Regional analysis, product-market matrices

```
Regional Sales Performance: 12 Regions × 8 Products
(Color intensity = revenue in millions)

Regions: North America, South America, Western Europe, Eastern Europe,
         Middle East, Africa, India, China, Japan, South Korea,
         Southeast Asia, Australia/NZ

Products: Product A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H
```

### 4. Sankey Diagrams
**Best for:** Flow analysis, customer journeys, resource allocation

```
Customer Acquisition Journey 2024

100,000 Total Leads
├─ 75,000 Qualified Leads
│  ├─ 30,000 Product A Interest
│  │  ├─ 15,000 Closed Won
│  │  └─ 15,000 Closed Lost
│  ├─ 25,000 Product B Interest  
│  │  ├─ 12,500 Closed Won
│  │  └─ 12,500 Closed Lost
│  └─ 20,000 Multi-Product
│     ├─ 14,000 Closed Won
│     └─ 6,000 Closed Lost
└─ 25,000 Disqualified
```

### 5. Treemap
**Best for:** Portfolio composition, hierarchical breakdowns

```
Investment Portfolio: $2.5B Total AUM

Tech Sector: $1.2B (48%)
├─ SaaS: $600M
│  ├─ Company A: $250M
│  ├─ Company B: $200M
│  └─ Company C: $150M
├─ Cloud Infrastructure: $400M
└─ Cybersecurity: $200M

Healthcare: $800M (32%)
├─ Biotech: $450M
└─ Medical Devices: $350M

Fintech: $500M (20%)
├─ Payments: $300M
└─ Lending: $200M
```

### 6. Multi-Series Line Chart
**Best for:** Trend comparisons over time

```
Revenue Growth: 5 Product Lines (2020-2024)
15-20 data points per line (quarterly data)

Lines:
- Product A: Growth from $50M to $180M (260% growth)
- Product B: Growth from $40M to $120M (200% growth)
- Product C: Growth from $30M to $95M (217% growth)
- Product D: Flat at $60M-$65M
- Product E: Launch in 2022, now at $45M
```

### 7. Bubble Chart
**Best for:** 3-dimensional analysis

```
Market Positioning Analysis

X-axis: Annual Revenue ($M)
Y-axis: Growth Rate (%)
Bubble Size: Market Share (%)

Data points for 12 companies showing:
- Position in market
- Growth trajectory
- Relative market dominance
```

### 8. Multi-Series Area Chart
**Best for:** Cumulative trends, market share evolution

```
Market Share Evolution: 2020-2024
(5 competitors, quarterly data points)

Shows stacked areas representing:
- Each competitor's market share over time
- Total market growth
- Shifts in competitive dynamics
```

## 💡 Data Point Requirements

### Detailed Mode Standards

**Time Series:**
- Minimum: 12-15 data points
- Ideal: 15-20+ data points
- Example: Quarterly data over 4-5 years

**Comparisons:**
- Minimum: 6-8 categories
- Ideal: 10-12 categories
- Use multi-series when comparing multiple entities

**Hierarchical Data:**
- 2-3 levels deep minimum
- Show actual values at each level
- Use treemap or sunburst

## 📏 Data Quality Standards

### Investment Banking-Grade Data

**Specificity:**
✅ "Revenue grew from $450M (2023) to $520M (2024), driven by $85M in new customer acquisition"
❌ "Revenue grew significantly last year"

**Citations:**
✅ "Market size: $127B (Gartner, 2024)"  
❌ "Large market opportunity"

**Calculations:**
✅ "CAGR of 23.4% from $50M (2020) to $180M (2024)"
❌ "Strong growth trend"

**Breakdowns:**
✅ Show all components: North America $45M, Europe $32M, Asia $18M = $95M total
❌ "Global revenue of $95M"

## 🎯 Chart Selection Guide

| Analysis Type | Best Chart | Data Points |
|--------------|------------|-------------|
| Revenue drivers | Waterfall | 6-10 components |
| Competitive comparison | Radar | 5 players × 6-8 metrics |
| Regional performance | Heatmap | 8-12 regions × 4-8 products |
| Customer journey | Sankey | 3-5 stages with flows |
| Portfolio composition | Treemap | 2-3 levels, 10-20 entities |
| Market share over time | Multi-series area | 15-20 quarters |
| Financial scenarios | Multi-line | 3-5 scenarios × 12-20 periods |
| Risk/Return analysis | Bubble or Scatter | 10-15 investments |

## 🚀 Implementation Tips

1. **Always include real numbers** - No placeholders or generic values
2. **Use consistent units** - All revenue in millions, all percentages as numbers
3. **Add 10-20+ data points** for time series - Don't under-populate charts
4. **Multi-series when comparing** - Show 3-5 entities simultaneously
5. **Include calculations** - Show how numbers are derived
6. **Cite sources** - Add footnotes for external data
7. **Use section headers** - Organize content with ## headers
8. **Multi-level bullets** - Use 2-space indents for hierarchical data

## ✨ Example Slide Content (Detailed Mode)

```markdown
## Revenue Growth Analysis

• Total revenue increased **$70M (15.6%)** from $450M to $520M in 2024
  • New customer acquisition contributed **$85M** (45 new enterprise customers)
  • Existing customer expansion added **$42M** (28% net revenue retention)
  • Customer churn impact: **-$35M** (primarily SMB segment)
  • Price optimization delivered **$18M** (avg 4.2% price increase)

## Growth Drivers by Segment

• **Enterprise segment** ($280M, 54% of revenue):
  • Q1: $65M, Q2: $68M, Q3: $72M, Q4: $75M
  • YoY growth: **21.3%** vs **$231M in 2023**
  
• **Mid-market** ($165M, 32% of revenue):
  • Consistent $40-42M per quarter
  • YoY growth: **13.8%** vs **$145M in 2023**
  
• **SMB segment** ($75M, 14% of revenue):
  • Decline from $82M in 2023 (**-8.5%**)
  • Strategic shift toward higher-value customers

## Regional Performance

• North America: **$312M (60%)**, growth **18.2%**
• Europe: **$145M (28%)**, growth **14.1%**  
• Asia-Pacific: **$63M (12%)**, growth **8.6%**

**Chart:** Revenue Waterfall showing the complete bridge from $450M → $520M
```
