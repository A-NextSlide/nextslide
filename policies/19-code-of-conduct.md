## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the ethical standards, professional behavior expectations, and personal conduct requirements for all individuals working at or on behalf of NextSlide. As an AI-powered presentation generation platform trusted by users to process their ideas and content through advanced AI systems (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT-4, Google Gemini), NextSlide holds a heightened responsibility to operate with integrity, transparency, and respect for user trust. This policy ensures that every member of the NextSlide team upholds the values that sustain that trust.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All NextSlide employees, regardless of role, seniority, or employment status (full-time, part-time, temporary).
- All contractors, consultants, and freelancers engaged by NextSlide.
- All interns and volunteers.
- All individuals representing NextSlide in any professional capacity, including at conferences, in open-source communities, on social media, or in communications with users, partners, and vendors (Supabase, Render, Modal, Stripe, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Sentry, PostHog, Cloudflare).

This policy applies during all work hours, at all NextSlide-sponsored events, and in any context where an individual's conduct could reasonably be associated with NextSlide.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Ethical Conduct** | Behavior that is honest, fair, transparent, and consistent with applicable laws, regulations, and the standards set forth in this policy. |
| **Conflict of Interest** | A situation in which an individual's personal interests, financial or otherwise, could improperly influence or appear to influence their professional judgment or actions on behalf of NextSlide. |
| **Confidential Information** | Any non-public information classified as CRITICAL, CONFIDENTIAL, or INTERNAL under the Data Classification and Handling Policy (NEXTSLIDE-POL-06), including user data, trade secrets, business strategies, and system architecture details. |
| **Harassment** | Unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic (race, gender, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, national origin, or any other protected class) that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment. |
| **Whistleblower** | An individual who reports suspected violations of this Code of Conduct, other NextSlide policies, or applicable laws, in good faith and through authorized reporting channels. |
| **Company Resources** | All NextSlide-owned or licensed assets including hardware, software, cloud infrastructure (Render, Supabase, Modal), SaaS accounts (Sentry, PostHog, Stripe), source code, intellectual property, and data. |
| **Retaliation** | Any adverse action taken against an individual because they reported a concern, participated in an investigation, or exercised their rights under this policy. |

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Integrity and Ethical Values

All NextSlide personnel shall conduct themselves with honesty, integrity, and transparency in all professional interactions. NextSlide's mission is to democratize professional presentation creation through AI, and that mission is only achievable if users, partners, and the broader community trust us to act ethically. Personnel shall:

- Be truthful and accurate in all business communications, documentation, and representations.
- Honor commitments made to users, colleagues, partners, and vendors.
- Make decisions based on the best interests of NextSlide and its users, not personal gain.
- Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and contractual obligations.
- Uphold the security and privacy of user data as described in the Information Security Policy (NEXTSLIDE-POL-01) and Data Classification and Handling Policy (NEXTSLIDE-POL-06).

### 4.2 Professional Behavior

NextSlide expects all personnel to maintain a professional standard of behavior that fosters a productive, respectful, and collaborative work environment. Personnel shall:

- Treat all colleagues, users, partners, and vendors with respect and dignity.
- Communicate constructively, especially during code reviews, incident response, and cross-functional collaboration.
- Be punctual and reliable in fulfilling their responsibilities.
- Maintain the quality and security of the NextSlide platform, including adherence to secure development practices as defined in NEXTSLIDE-POL-17.
- Seek to resolve disagreements through respectful dialogue and established escalation paths.

### 4.3 Conflicts of Interest

Personnel must avoid situations where personal interests conflict or appear to conflict with their duties to NextSlide. Potential conflicts of interest include but are not limited to:

- Holding a financial interest in a NextSlide competitor or in any vendor (Supabase, Render, Modal, Stripe, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) where that interest could influence professional decisions.
- Accepting gifts, hospitality, or compensation from vendors, partners, or users that could influence or appear to influence professional judgment. Nominal gifts (under $100 in value) exchanged in normal business courtesy are permitted.
- Engaging in outside employment or consulting that competes with NextSlide or that utilizes NextSlide proprietary information, source code, or infrastructure.
- Hiring, supervising, or making business decisions involving family members or close personal relations without disclosure and approval.

All actual or potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed in writing to the CEO. The CEO shall determine whether a conflict exists and prescribe any required mitigations, which may include recusal from specific decisions.

### 4.4 Confidentiality Obligations

All personnel have a duty to protect NextSlide's confidential information as defined in the Data Classification and Handling Policy (NEXTSLIDE-POL-06). This obligation persists during employment and after separation. Specifically, personnel shall:

- Never disclose CRITICAL data (Supabase service keys, DATABASE_URL, API keys for Anthropic/OpenAI/Google, STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, SLACK_TOKEN_ENCRYPTION_KEY) to unauthorized individuals, inside or outside NextSlide.
- Never access, copy, or distribute CONFIDENTIAL user data (presentation content, email addresses, payment metadata, Slack OAuth tokens) except as required by their specific job responsibilities.
- Never discuss INTERNAL operational details (Sentry error data, audit logs, deployment configurations, rate limiting thresholds) with external parties without CTO approval.
- Secure all devices, accounts, and credentials used to access NextSlide systems.
- Return or destroy all NextSlide confidential information upon termination of employment or engagement, per the offboarding procedures in NEXTSLIDE-POL-20.

### 4.5 Acceptable Use of Company Resources

NextSlide provides resources to enable personnel to perform their duties effectively. These resources must be used responsibly and in accordance with the Acceptable Use Policy (NEXTSLIDE-POL-12). Personnel shall:

- Use NextSlide cloud infrastructure (Render, Supabase, Modal), SaaS accounts (Sentry, PostHog, Stripe dashboard), and development tools solely for authorized business purposes.
- Not use NextSlide AI provider API keys (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) for personal projects or unauthorized purposes, as such usage directly incurs costs to NextSlide.
- Not install unauthorized software or services on NextSlide infrastructure or connect personal devices to production systems without explicit approval.
- Not use NextSlide systems to store, transmit, or process illegal, offensive, or inappropriate content.
- Report any suspected misuse of company resources to their manager or the CTO.

Limited personal use of company resources (e.g., checking personal email) is permitted provided it does not interfere with job responsibilities, consume material resources, or create security risks.

### 4.6 Reporting Violations and Whistleblower Protections

NextSlide is committed to maintaining an environment where personnel feel safe reporting concerns without fear of retaliation. Personnel who become aware of actual or suspected violations of this Code of Conduct, other NextSlide policies, or applicable laws shall:

- Report the concern promptly through one of the following channels:
  - Directly to their manager.
  - Directly to the CEO.
  - Directly to the CTO (for technical and security-related concerns).
  - Via a written report to the designated compliance contact.
- Provide as much factual detail as possible to facilitate investigation.

**Whistleblower protections:** NextSlide strictly prohibits retaliation against any individual who reports a concern in good faith. Retaliation includes but is not limited to termination, demotion, reduction in compensation, reassignment to less favorable duties, harassment, or any other adverse action motivated by the reporting of a concern. Any individual who engages in retaliation shall be subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination. Good faith reporting is protected even if the reported concern is ultimately not substantiated.

### 4.7 Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination

NextSlide is committed to providing a work environment free from harassment and discrimination. All personnel shall:

- Treat others with respect regardless of race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.
- Refrain from making derogatory comments, slurs, jokes, or gestures related to any protected characteristic.
- Refrain from unwelcome physical contact, sexual advances, or requests for sexual favors.
- Not create, display, or distribute offensive or discriminatory material in the workplace or through company communication channels.
- Report any witnessed or experienced harassment or discrimination through the reporting channels defined in Section 4.6.

All reports of harassment or discrimination shall be investigated promptly, thoroughly, and impartially. Confidentiality shall be maintained to the greatest extent possible. Individuals found to have engaged in harassment or discrimination shall be subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.

### 4.8 Social Media and Public Communications

Personnel must exercise good judgment when using social media or making public statements that could be associated with NextSlide. The following guidelines apply:

- Do not disclose CRITICAL, CONFIDENTIAL, or INTERNAL information (as classified in NEXTSLIDE-POL-06) on social media or in public forums, including technical implementation details of Supabase RLS policies, security header configurations, rate limiting thresholds, or incident details.
- When expressing personal opinions on platforms where your affiliation with NextSlide is known, include a disclaimer that the views are personal and do not represent NextSlide.
- Do not engage in public disputes with users, competitors, or partners on behalf of NextSlide unless authorized by the CEO.
- Do not share screenshots or recordings of internal NextSlide tools, dashboards (Sentry, PostHog, Stripe, Supabase), or communications without explicit approval.
- Refer media inquiries and press requests to the CEO.

### 4.9 AI Ethics and Responsible Use

Given NextSlide's core business of AI-powered presentation generation using Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT-4, and Google Gemini, all personnel involved in AI system development and deployment shall:

- Ensure that AI features are designed and operated transparently, with users informed that AI generates or assists with content.
- Not intentionally train, fine-tune, or configure AI systems to produce misleading, discriminatory, or harmful content.
- Report any observed AI system outputs that are biased, harmful, or inconsistent with NextSlide's values to the engineering team for investigation and remediation.
- Respect the terms of service and acceptable use policies of AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google).
- Protect user presentation content submitted to AI providers by ensuring data processing agreements are in place and that content is not retained beyond the scope of the request.

### 4.10 Compliance with Laws and Regulations

All personnel must comply with applicable laws and regulations in the jurisdictions where NextSlide operates. This includes but is not limited to data protection and privacy laws, intellectual property laws, anti-corruption laws, export control regulations, and employment laws. When in doubt about the legality of an action, personnel should consult the CEO or seek legal counsel before proceeding.

## 7. Compliance and Enforcement

All personnel are required to comply with this Code of Conduct as a condition of their employment or engagement with NextSlide. Violations shall be addressed as follows:

- **Minor violations** (first-time, unintentional, limited impact): Verbal or written counseling, with documentation retained in the personnel file.
- **Moderate violations** (repeated minor violations, negligent conduct, or conduct causing moderate harm): Written warning with a defined corrective action plan and timeline.
- **Severe violations** (intentional misconduct, violations involving CRITICAL data, harassment, discrimination, retaliation, fraud, or illegal activity): Suspension pending investigation, with potential outcomes including termination and referral to law enforcement where appropriate.

The CEO shall make final determinations on disciplinary actions, with input from the CTO on technical matters. All investigations shall be conducted promptly, impartially, and with appropriate confidentiality.

## 8. Exceptions

There are no blanket exceptions to the ethical standards and professional conduct expectations defined in this policy. In rare circumstances where a specific provision of this policy conflicts with applicable law or creates an unresolvable practical difficulty, personnel may request a limited exception from the CEO. Such requests must be in writing, explain the specific conflict, and propose alternative measures to uphold the policy's intent. Approved exceptions shall be documented and time-limited.

## 9. Review Schedule

This policy shall be reviewed:

- Annually, on or before the Next Review Date.
- Following any significant Code of Conduct investigation or finding that reveals gaps in the policy.
- Upon material changes to NextSlide's business model, organizational structure, or AI provider relationships.
- Upon changes to applicable employment, anti-discrimination, or data protection laws.
